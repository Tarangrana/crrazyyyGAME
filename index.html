Forgotten Acorns

A squirrel's year in four seasons. Every acorn you forget becomes a tree.

How to play

You're a squirrel. Keep your food bar full and live through 3 winters.

1. Fall: collect and bury

Acorns fall from the trees. Run over them to grab them, then press ↓ to bury them.

2. Winter: dig and eat

Nothing grows. Go to your buried acorns (the snowy mounds) and press ↓ to dig one up and eat it.

3. Spring: trees grow

Every acorn you didn't dig up grows into a new tree. More trees means more acorns next fall.

4. Summer: berries and hawks

Run into bushes to eat berries. If a red ring appears under you, a hawk is coming, so stand under a tree.
←→ move ↑ or Space jump ↓ bury / dig Esc pause

You eat acorns from your cheeks automatically when you get hungry. The line at the top of the screen always tells you what to do right now.
