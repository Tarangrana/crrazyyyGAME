Forgotten Acorns

A squirrel's year in four seasons. Every acorn you forget becomes a tree.

Survive three winters

Bury acorns in Fall. Dig them up in Winter to eat. Any cache you never dig up sprouts in Spring into an oak, which drops more acorns the next Fall. The seasons change the rules:

Spring

Forgotten caches sprout into saplings. Rain floods the stream, so you have to swim. Mushrooms pop up. Hawks hunt: hide under leaves or in your den.

Summer

Berries everywhere, but the sun makes you hungry faster, so stay in the shade. Hawks hunt more often. The stream dries up.

Fall

Oaks drop acorns. Your cheeks hold 3, so bury the rest. Wind gusts push you around. Blue jays raid any cache they can see, so chase them off. Geese mean winter is near.

Winter

Nothing grows. Snow hides your caches, so remember where they are or sniff for them. The ground is frozen: you can only dig up, not bury. Warm up in your den. The stream freezes into slippery ice.

The more you forget, the bigger the forest: more acorns, more shade, more cover from hawks. But forget too much and you'll starve.
